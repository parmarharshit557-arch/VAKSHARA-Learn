Binary numbers and bitwise operators

Bitwise operators integers ke binary bits par kaam karte hain. AND, OR, XOR, NOT ya shift lagane se pehle bits ko position ke hisaab se align karke dekhein.

Real-life example

Do rows mein chhote switches socho. Har switch OFF (0) ya ON (1) hota hai. Bitwise operator same position wale switches compare karta hai.

Step-by-step samjhaav

  1. 5 ko 4 bits mein 0101 aur 3 ko 0011 likhte hain.
  2. AND mein dono bits 1 hon tabhi 1 milta hai: 0001 = 1.
  3. OR mein kisi ek bit ke 1 hone par 1 milta hai: 0111 = 7. XOR different bits par 1 deta hai: 0110 = 6.

Python example

a = 5       # binary: 0101
b = 3       # binary: 0011
print(a & b)
print(a | b)
print(a ^ b)

Expected output

1
7
6

Common mistake

and/or Boolean logic hain; &/| bitwise operators hain. Dono alag hain.

Yaad rakho

AND = dono; OR = kam se kam ek; XOR = different. Calculation se pehle bits align karo.
